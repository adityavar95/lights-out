# 3D stack and look

3D stack (three.js 0.187 WebGPURenderer + TSL, no WebGL fallback path written for it; `AUDIT_AND_MASTER_PLAN.md` has the migration record, and this paragraph wins where the module notes below still describe the old WebGL chain): one renderer with a `CanvasTarget` per canvas (`scene3d.js` main, `showroom.js` via `setDeck`; a target only hears its resize while current, so the showroom resizes inside its draw), `post.js` one `RenderPipeline` graph with four tiers (G) ending in AgX plus Blender's Punchy look (contrast and saturation after the curve), `CSMShadowNode` sun, HDRI sky presets (L); Settings' sky defaults to Auto: `scene3d.js` `setDaylight` picks Midday / Golden hour / Night from the sun's elevation over the circuit at the session's start (`weather.js` `sunElevation`, called by `replay.js` `daylight` once the race row and again with a GPS replay's clock start; pre-2005 races have no Jolpica time and stay Midday) and Overcast while `setWeather` says a daytime track is wet; a replay's weather look is `weather.js` `replayWeather` (water builds under OpenF1's rain flag and drains ~25 min after, and a field mostly on intermediates or wets says wet before any flag; `pitwall.js` `tyresOut`), the Simulation's its own `weather.js` state. Depth bias is a fraction of the shadow camera's range: too large and cars cast nothing. `scene3d.js` `softShadow` is a fixed 3x3 tent (three's PCF rotates its taps by per-pixel noise: grain), set on `sun.shadow.clone` since a clone drops `filterNode`; the far two cascades redraw every 2nd and 4th frame. `post.js` `aoBlur` denoises GTAO, which leaves that to the caller. No `:has()` in the page's styles without measuring: `.mvbar:has(details[open])` re-styled ~700 elements on every 3D frame, viewer shut. The velocity attachment blends by its own alpha, and every effect (`vfx.js` `noVelocity`) writes alpha 0: a transparent material otherwise overwrites all MRT attachments, and a smoke puff's single vector smeared whatever lay under it. New: `trackside.js` (instanced stanchions, Tecpro, boards, floodlights, marshal posts, 18-row stands with a cantilever roof; `clearOf` removes stands on the pit lane), `teledrawer.js` + pure `pitdata.js` (T), `showroom.js` (C), `loadstage.js` (loading percentages). `settings.js` is the stage's Settings button and panel (top right; sound moved there from the ⋯ menu), `graphics.js` its pure presets, defaults and validation (localStorage `lo-graphics`, `lo-audio`), `scene3d.js` `setGraphics` what applies them: a change of MSAA makes a new `createPost` (its sample count is built into the pass and every pipeline), and `post.js` frees each rebuild's render targets. Keys: 1-5 cameras, [ ] driver, Shift+T the trace. `agent-browser console` doesn't show uncaught exceptions: add a window `error` listener when testing.

Look (Modern Dark type and palette under glisse's glass, wins where the notes below say system UI face or red): `--dp-*` tokens at the top of `style.css`, Geist + Geist Mono (HUD readouts and uppercase labels; data-table figures (`.num`) are Geist with tabular-nums, as F1 timing sets them), near-black ground with soft white light behind the page (`body::before`, so frost has something to catch), glass cards, white primary actions, 6 px panels and pill buttons, mint `--dp-color-live` only on live marks, images always in full colour. `--accent` is white in dark: text on it takes `--accent-ink`. Team colours (header swatch or palette, `app.js` `setTeam`, localStorage `f1team`) set both inline on `:root`, the ink black or white by `util.js` `inkOn`; real red flags and the LO badge take `--flag-red`. Legible glass: every lens and frost ends its backdrop-filter with `--lg-legible` (style.css top), after Apple's regular Liquid Glass: contrast + brightness squeeze the backdrop into a band (dark ~10-60 %, light ~26-100 %) under the tint, so text holds over white art or a black stage; subtrees that are always dark (Stream, HUD, viewer, globe panes) redeclare it with `--glass-blur`, which resolves where declared. Glass settings are glisse's custom properties, read per surface by `glass.js`: `--lg-glass-bend`, `--lg-glass-dispersion` (0 over the 3D stage), `--lg-blur`, `--lg-rim-shine`. Stream's cards and buttons are liquid panes: bevel `--lg-bevel` and `#lg-liquid` (noise displacement) after a 21st.dev liquid-glass button, over the billboard art blurred behind the rows (`.stream::before`); lenses go off out of view (`data-lg-far`) and fall back to frost while anything scrolls (`html.lg-moving`), and an ancestor's `mask` would cut them off from the backdrop. The top bar floats as a glass pill, Races / Simulate / Stream at its true centre on every page (a centred row of their own under 1140 px) (its glass on `.topbar::before`: a backdrop-filter on the bar would leave its pills see-through). The rim is brighter in dark (`glass.js` SHINE .3) and `--lg-spec` (style.css `:root`: light along the top-left edge, a faint inner glow) lights the bar's glass and the globe's panes (`.ghint`, `.gplay`, `.gclose`: lenses over the globe, tint `rgb(10 14 26 / .5)` so text holds over bright imagery; the pins' name chips are plain translucent, 20 lenses over a moving canvas would cost frames). Type floor 12 px; spacing and radius on the redesign skill's scale (2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 24...). Waits are `util.js` `skeleton`, except the replay overlay and tile rings, which say what's happening. The light theme stays behind the switch; dark is the default.

Frame budget (Nov 2026; measured, see `docs/graphics-60fps-plan.md`): the frame is limited by the main thread's draw calls, then by pixels. `scene3d.js` `timed` / `frameStats` keep each part's milliseconds (scene, draw, tags, sim, page, `gpu` from timestamp queries, and `rest`: the frame-to-frame time none of those cover, which is the page's own layout, style and paint plus waiting on vsync or the GPU), shown by Settings' frame rate counter. A car's hidden LOD level gets no matrix update (`ownLods`, `whenShown`), and the loop picks the levels itself before the matrices. Cars cast shadows through merged casters on `cars3d.js` `SHADOW_LAYER`, which only the sun's cameras see: one for a far car, five for the RB22 (body and each wheel); the showroom's own lamps don't see that layer, so its cars keep per-part casters. The RB22 shows within 60 m (40 m on a weak GPU). The cockpit's two mirrors redraw one a frame, in turn. Resolution Auto is a governor on GPU time: down over 8 ms, back up under 4, each step down doubling the wait before the next try up; 75 % of the display's pixels is its floor. `frame()` reads no layout: the stage's size is the `ResizeObserver`'s.
